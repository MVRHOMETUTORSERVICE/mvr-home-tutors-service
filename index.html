Help

Frequently Asked Questions

Is email verification included?

Yes. The registration flow is designed to use email verification/OTP before the account is treated as verified.

Can I see my registration number after registering?

Yes. The registration success screen is designed to remain visible with your registration number, name, email and role. The same registration number should also be stored in the backend for future login and app use.

Can a tutor upload a resume and identity proof?

Yes. These are separate private document fields. They should be accessible only to authorized users.

Can I show or hide my password?

Yes. The password and confirm-password fields include Show/Hide controls.
